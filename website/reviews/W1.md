## W0 deviations for review

1. W1 labels the primary CTA **“Preview report”** and navigation **“Report preview”**.
   They lead to the static capture. “Open the actual report” becomes “View full-size
   capture”. This avoids presenting W2 functionality as available. The CTA emphasis,
   placement and approved composition remain intact.
2. The decorative theme icon becomes a labeled native System/Light/Dark selector;
   the mobile menu becomes a keyboard-operable native disclosure. Mobile header
   height is 86px to fit usable controls. No-JavaScript navigation still works.
3. Small mobile labels are at least 12px; section eyebrows are 11px English / 12px
   Arabic. Light-theme legal text and code line numbers are slightly darker for contrast. Page heights
   differ from the fixed compositions because of readable labels and real controls.
4. The W0 mobile “graph” crop contained overview counts due to a screenshot-coordinate
   error. It now shows the actual selected backend node and source line. Light
   mobile captures use the released light viewer. Captures are explicitly static,
   cropped and English; the separate website summary says what it is.
5. Header/footer logos use the already-approved 320px display variants, byte-for-byte,
   rather than loading 640px images at small sizes. Appearance/proportions are retained.
   Fonts are local WOFF2 subsets; the existing W0 monospace family is now local too.
6. Tablet widths and 320px use responsive wrapping. An Arabic glow pseudo-element
   overflow at 320px was corrected. Code excerpts have their own keyboard-accessible
   horizontal scroll region; the document itself does not scroll horizontally.
7. The larger export-format cards and closer desktop graph framing are explicitly
   approved W1 adaptations from the later W0 compositions, including their use on
   the English-dark homepage. They are retained; no visual exploration or baseline
   replacement was performed during remediation.
